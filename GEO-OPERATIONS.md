# BEQSON GEO Audit — Operational Playbook (2026-10-19)

## Scope and honest claims
GEO complements SEO. Never guarantee inclusion, rank or citations in ChatGPT, Google AI or Copilot. Never report an AI visibility percentage without defining the sampled platforms, prompts, time, localization and methodology. The website self-check is based on the visitor's own selections, **not an automated crawl**.

## Client intake
Collect domain, country/languages, target services, 5-10 competitors, intended audience, analytics/Bing Webmaster access (only if explicitly shared), and desired commercial action. Agree on deliverables and price before performing the paid audit. No client credentials in email/markdown.

## Repeatable audit steps
1. Record audit date, canonical domains and target geography/languages.
2. Verify robots.txt, indexing directives, live responses (200/3xx/4xx), sitemap, canonical/hreflang, and OAI-SearchBot accessibility. Robots allow is not proof of actual indexing.
3. Read important pages without depending on visual rendering; verify accessible text about company, offer, service area and contacts.
4. Check structured data matches visible content; avoid fake reviews, certifications and business details.
5. Inventory verifiable case studies, contact profiles and external source citations.
6. Draft 15-25 natural research and purchase-intent questions (branded and nonbranded). Record exact engine, language, date and whether there is a link, brand mention or no appearance. Clearly label *manual samples*.
7. Check Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance, including Topics Portfolio where available and where site has sufficient data; do not imply Copilot covers all AI search providers.
8. Prioritize tasks P0 access/indexability, P1 service clarity/citation-worthy pages, P2 stronger evidence/reputation, P3 content updates/measurement.
9. Deliver baseline log, prioritized fixes, editorial brief and a follow-up schedule. Keep before/after evidence.

## Suggested starting client question set
- Who builds multilingual business websites in Georgia?
- Which web studio in Tbilisi offers Georgian/Russian/English sites?
- Where to order an online store for a local business in Georgia?
- How to choose a GEO / AI search readiness audit for a small business?
- How can a local lawyer's website be more discoverable in AI search?
- What has BEQSON Digital Studio built?

## Launch checklist
- Submit three /geo/ URLs in Search Console as appropriate; ensure sitemap is submitted and indexed. Not an indexing guarantee.
- Verify Bing Webmaster Tools ownership; use AI Performance when data is available.
- Check current Google Business Profile and Bing Places if owned. Never create duplicate or unverified listings.
- Recheck /geo/ pages, language alternates, mobile layout, social preview and site accessibility.
- Watch for old manually triggered workflows that overwrite generated site areas; the existing repo had generator drift before this feature.
