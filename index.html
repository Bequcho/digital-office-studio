FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to understand websites or SEO?

No. This is a done-for-you setup. You provide accurate information about your business; the technical work is handled for you.

Can I keep my existing domain or Google profile?

Yes. Existing assets can be improved and connected instead of rebuilt when that makes sense.

Do you guarantee a certain number of customers?

No. Demand, competition, pricing and reputation vary. We build and improve the acquisition infrastructure, but we do not promise a fixed number of leads or sales.

Can the site be multilingual?

Yes. Georgian, Russian and English versions can be built when they match your real audience.
