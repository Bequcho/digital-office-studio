ABOUT THE PROJECT

More than a beautiful storefront.

FORME HOME is a portfolio concept built with WordPress and WooCommerce. The interactive demo showcases a product catalog, product detail pages, cart and a test-only checkout in a temporary personal browser environment.

01Product catalog

16 sample products organized into home decor and furniture categories.

02Working cart

Explore products and update item quantities using WooCommerce.

03Test checkout

A demo order flow with real payment methods disabled.

04Responsive layout

A custom bright editorial theme for desktops, tablets and phones.
