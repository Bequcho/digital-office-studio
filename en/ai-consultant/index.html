COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the knowledge base, languages, platforms and integration requirements. We quote after a short discovery call or brief.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. We check your current website and platform before recommending the right integration.

Will it automatically write to my CRM?

Not by default. CRM, calendar and lead-capture integrations need their own configuration and testing; they are not presented as working features of this demo.

Can I try a live example?

Yes. The embedded assistant here is our public working demo, and a full-screen version is available.
