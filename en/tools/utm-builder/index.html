HOW UTM PARAMETERS WORK
utm_sourceTraffic source: google, instagram, telegram, newsletter.
utm_mediumChannel type: cpc, social, email, referral.
utm_campaignCampaign name: autumn-sale, lawyer-launch, black-friday.
utm_content / utm_termOptional detail for ads, buttons, creatives or keywords.
BEQSON tip

Use one naming convention across all campaigns. Otherwise analytics may treat “Google”, “google” and “GOOGLE” as different traffic sources.
