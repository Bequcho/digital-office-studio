FAQ

Medical website
questions.

Practical answers before launching a website for a doctor or private clinic.

What should a clinic website include?

Doctors, specialties, services, locations, working information, contact methods and a simple appointment path.

Can every doctor have a profile?

Yes. Doctor pages can include specialty, qualifications, languages, locations and other verified professional details.

Do you help with local SEO?

Yes — location structure, metadata, internal links and local-search foundations. We never promise a specific Google position.

How much does it cost?

International launch pricing starts from $149. The final price depends on doctors, services, languages, SEO, booking integrations and CRM scope.
