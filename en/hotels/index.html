FAQ

Hotel website
questions.

Practical answers for hotels, apartments and tourism businesses planning a stronger direct web presence.

What should a hotel website include?

Room or property pages, strong visuals, amenities, location details, mobile usability and clear enquiry or booking actions.

Can you add direct booking?

We can build direct enquiry flows and connect supported third-party booking engines or reservation links where available.

Can the site target destinations in Google?

Yes. We can structure useful pages around destinations, neighbourhoods and accommodation types as part of the SEO plan.

How much does it cost?

International launch pricing starts from $149. Languages, room types, booking integrations, CRM and SEO scope determine the final price.
