01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designed to make a feeling tangible.

Family Voices is an independent speculative advertising project created by BEQSON Digital Studio. Inspired by the Remento product idea—preserving spoken family stories alongside a printed keepsake—the campaign explores how emotional storytelling can translate into clear, premium static ads for social feeds and vertical placements. No work was commissioned by Remento, and these ads have not been used in a campaign.

02 / CREATIVE CHALLENGE

Emotion first. Product clarity close behind.

CHALLENGE

The creative challenge

Communicate an intangible benefit—the irreplaceable sound of a loved one's voice—within a static frame that can be understood in seconds. Each direction needed a simple hook, expressive imagery, legible typography and a focused call to action.

INSIGHT

Audience and emotional insight

Hypothetical audience: adult children looking for meaningful gifts for parents, families documenting grandparents' memories, and people who value personal archives. Creative hypothesis: specificity and genuine human connection can make the message resonate more deeply than generic nostalgia. This is a design assumption, not audience research or a tested claim.

03 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Warmth, tactility and restraint.

A consistent editorial system anchors all six ads: warm cinematic photography, paper and linen textures, serif headlines with modern sans-serif supporting copy, elegant spacing, and a quiet palette of ivory, walnut, olive, cocoa and muted gold. Distinct compositions explore intimate portraiture, real family connection, premium gifting and product demonstration.

04 / ADVERTISING CONCEPTS

Three angles. Two treatments each.

A different emotional or functional reason to care in every group—not simply a change of color or crop.

CONCEPT 01

Preserve Their Voice

The human voice becomes the emotional centerpiece. One treatment uses a shared family moment; the other focuses on a grandfather's quiet storytelling presence.

Independent art direction · 4:5 feed layout
CONCEPT 02

A Gift That Lasts

A keepsake as a considered, deeply personal gift. The two treatments contrast the emotional moment of giving with a tactile premium product still life.

Independent art direction · 4:5 feed layout
CONCEPT 03

Read the Story. Hear the Voice.

The product mechanism made visible: a printed memory and the possibility of hearing the original voice. QR and audio-player elements are illustrative, not working product screens.

Independent art direction · 4:5 feed layout

06 / FORMAT ADAPTATION

Designed for feed and full screen.

Each visual direction was separately arranged for 1080 × 1920 px vertical placements, with headline, CTA and disclosure kept clear of the main interface zones where practical. Final media placements should always be previewed inside the ad platform.

07 / DESIGN WORKFLOW

A repeatable creative production process.

01

Message & insight

Define one emotional promise, a simple advertising hook and a clear CTA for each concept.

02

Visual exploration

Art-direct AI-assisted source photography and curate natural, believable scenes.

03

Type & composition

Set every line of copy deterministically; refine contrast, hierarchy and illustrative overlays.

04

Adapt & quality-check

Build separate 4:5 and 9:16 layouts; verify dimensions, readability, faces, artifacts and spelling.
