    05 / QUESTIONS

    GEO questions, clearly answered

    What is GEO compared with SEO?+

    GEO focuses on the discoverability, clarity and supporting evidence of information that AI search may retrieve. It complements, not replaces, foundational SEO.

    Can you guarantee my site is mentioned in ChatGPT?+

    No. Search and AI systems choose sources independently and results vary by time and prompt. We improve measurable factors and document observed outcomes.

    Can you calculate a universal AI visibility percentage?+

    No reliable universal score exists across AI platforms. We use documented prompt samples, citation checks and first-party platform reports where available.

    Do I need llms.txt or special GEO markup?+

    Google says special AI-specific files or markup are not required for AI Overviews. Structured data should represent content visible to human visitors.

    How is this different from BEQSON's free SEO audit?+

    The free SEO tool checks basic technical signals. GEO Audit adds manual research into the brand, answer coverage, source citations and an implementation roadmap.

    MEASUREMENT & TRANSPARENCY

    How we report progress

    For each observation we record the URL, date, platform, query and citation context. Bing AI Performance may provide page-level citations and topics; not every AI provider offers comparable reporting. A missing citation in a sample does not establish universal absence.

    First-party references

    Our process is grounded in official search engine guidance rather than promises about hidden ranking tricks.
